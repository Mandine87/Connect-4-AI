# Connect-4-AI

A clone of the Connect 4 made with Unity. You can play against your friends, against an AI or just watch 2 AI playing against each other. AI have 3 difficulty level. The AI were made with the Minimax algorithm.



Author : CELERIER Amandine

Finished the 01/03/2026



Gameplay : 



&#x09;Connect 4 is a game where you'll need to align 4 token of your color to win. You can play against a friend, 

an AI or watch 2 AI play together. You can select the AI difficulty (from EASY to HARD) for more challenge.



Controls :



&#x09;- Mouse : Press on a button

&#x09;- Q or Left : move the token to the left

&#x09;- D or Right : move the token to the rigth

&#x09;- Enter : Place the token or go to the end screen

&#x09;- Echap : Pause or Unpause the game
